ACTIVITY SEQUENCE

Gameplay and
learning objectives

The eight activities and their intended learning focus.

ActivityWhat the player doesIntended learning focus
1. Manual recommendationAsk limited questions and choose a tape for a customer.Preference elicitation, context, and the limits of manual service.
2. Rule-based recommendationConstruct genre-to-movie rules and inspect batch outcomes.Automation, brittle rules, and age-appropriate recommendations.
3. Ratings tablesInspect individual rating rows and compare column averages.Representing feedback and distinguishing individual taste from aggregate ratings.
4. Collaborative filteringCompare customers and predict missing ratings.Similarity-based inference and sparse observations.
5. Matrix factorizationAdjust latent-factor sliders, then run an optimizer.Hidden factors, prediction error, optimization, privacy, and popularity bias.
6. Content-based recommendationCompare customer preferences with movie features.Feature representations, explanations, and the limits of descriptive labels.
7. Market-gap researchCombine setting, character, story, and prop choices into a movie brief.Using audience evidence to support human creative decisions.
8. Poster generationRevise a prompt, choose a style, and compare generated drafts when the image service is connected.Iterative prompting and human evaluation of generated media.
